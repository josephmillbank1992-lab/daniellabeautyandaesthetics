# Website Mill standards — Daniella Grow

Confirmed 28 October 2026: client paid £300 for Grow, not Launch.

## Approved reference foundations
- Mane London: useful catalogue, tailored options, shortlist, guided enquiry, selections carried into contact and practical visit details.
- Tailored Tools: contextual proof, purposeful page progression and choices that reduce enquiry back-and-forth.
- Adapt these patterns to Daniella's business. Do not copy another business's visual identity, policies or irrelevant functionality.

## Design
- Distinct composition; do not repeat the arched/window hero from earlier client work.
- Real imagery and exact chosen logo. No fabricated face, results, reviews or student outcomes.
- Clear service language, course prices and prerequisites, evidence next to relevant actions.
- Avoid vague repeated aspirational headings and sparse placeholder panels.
- Warm neutral palette, approachable expertise; clinic and academy both meaningful.
- No developer/source/implementation language in public gallery copy.
- Responsive readable layouts, accessible keyboard controls, reduced-motion support.

## Daniella specifics
- Primary conversion: academy enquiries. Secondary: skin/aesthetics bookings.
- Dedicated course pages, comparison and experience filter.
- £775 combined kit-inclusive pathway highlighted; £595 training-only preserved.
- Clinic stays private: Derby only, never the full home address.
- Booking remains on Fresha. Training remains enquiry-first.
- Junior introduction is not an accredited qualification.
- 2027 award/judging references corrected to 2026 per Joe.
- Honest interim contact: user copies the message and sends through Instagram, until a receiving email or WhatsApp number is supplied.

## Daniella visual direction
- Editorial, academy-led story with layered imagery and her own supportive teaching voice.
- Avoid another symmetric cream hero and repeated static two-column sections.
- Motion must support the story and honour reduced-motion preference. Autoplay pauses on hover/focus and when the hero or page is inactive; do not add a visible pause-motion button.
- Source exact branding from the attached presentation, with concept choice documented.

## Readability and controls — 30 October 2026
- Joe explicitly requested removal of visible pause-motion controls, including the header and hero buttons.
- Preserve system reduced-motion support and automatic pause behaviour.
- Body text: 16px; supporting text: 14px; small labels: at least 12px. Do not shrink mobile labels to 5–10px.
